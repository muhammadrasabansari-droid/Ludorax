How to play
• Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the track.
• Tap one of your highlighted tokens to move it.
• Roll a 6 for another turn.
• Land on an opponent to send them home. Safe squares protect tokens.
• First player to get all 4 tokens home wins.

Note: This is a local pass-and-play version for one device.
